Prosperity built on kindness

Nhơn Thịnh cooks every meal as if for family, and runs every day to standards an international business can trust.

The story of our name

ThịnhProsperity that lasts.

A business only lasts when it is built on kindness

Nhơn Thịnh believes a business only prospers for long when it is built on kindness:

  • Kindness to clients, through every safe meal.
  • Kindness to employees, through clear hours and clear pay.
  • Kindness to partners, through transparency.
Vision 2030

The industrial meal partner trusted by FDI businesses, schools and international organisations in southern Vietnam

Through safety, transparency and sustainable operations.

For clients

For employees

A workplace with clear hours, transparent pay and room to grow.

For the community

Responsible about ingredient sourcing, the environment and diners’ health.
